Activity Tracker

This PC's part of Reem. It counts how the workday is spent on this computer and sends the day to HR.

What is recorded

  • Which program and window is in front, and for how long
  • When there is no mouse or keyboard for over 3 minutes (idle)
  • When the PC is locked or asleep (away)

What is not

  • No screenshots, no keystrokes, no camera
  • No files, no emails, no messages are read

Where it goes

The detail stays on this PC and is deleted after 30 days. At the end of each day a summary (first and last activity, time per program) goes to Reem, and HR receives one daily report for everyone.
