# Project Planner: one file in, a full plan out, kept up to date from site

*Plan, 3 November 2026. Not built yet. Updated the same day with the decisions below.*

---

## Part A: the idea in plain words

### 1. You start it with one file
In a chat, you (master) attach **one file**, for example the full drawing set of the JVC school (PDF, up to 100 pages / 25 MB, or a DWG up to 35 MB), and say:

> "Make the full plan for this project."

### 2. Reem reads everything in the file
From the JVC drawings, for example, it takes:
- project name, plot, owner, consultant, approval and its date,
- floors and levels (Basement −4.10 … Roof +20.20),
- staircases, lifts, rooms and their use,
- window and door schedules (types, sizes, fire ratings),
- parking, bus bays, playground.

### 3. It asks only what the file doesn't say, all at once
For example: start date? budget? contractor? Do you have the structural / MEP drawings or the BOQ? You answer in one message, or attach those files too.

### 4. It writes the full plan and shows it on one card
| Phase | What Reem writes | Quality checks before the next phase |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Brief | Scope from the file, floors, areas, deadline, budget, who is who | Every floor and area named; dates and budget confirmed by you |
| 2. Programme | Steps and weeks per floor (concrete, blockwork, MEP, finishes) | No step without a week; hold points on every pour and cover-up |
| 3. Quality plan | Inspection points per stage (rebar before pour, cube tests, waterproofing, MEP pressure tests, fire doors as scheduled) | Each stage has a hold point and who signs it |
| 4. Materials | What is needed per floor and when to order it (from the schedules in the file) | Main materials ordered at least 2 weeks before they are needed |
| 5. Manpower | Trades and headcount per week | No week with work but no trade |
| 6. Risks | Top risks and what to do | Each risk has an owner and an action |

You tap **Start** (or say what to change). The steps go into the **JVC Project** you already have, and the full plan goes to your Shelf as a PDF.

### 5. Francisca and Arzhel keep it updated, from chat, as now
- "20 tons of rebar arrived", "ground floor slab poured today", "MEP stopped, no pipes".
- They confirm on the usual cards. They only report; they don't change the plan.

### 6. Reem watches the updates against the plan
"Late" means one of these:
1. A step hasn't started by its planned week.
2. A step isn't finished by its planned week.
3. A material isn't received in time.
4. Work went ahead without the hold-point check being signed.
5. Someone reported the work as blocked.

When that happens, Reem says what is late, by how much, what it affects, and proposes a fix ("blockwork moves to week 14 — accept?"). It buzzes **you, Francisca and Arzhel**. **Only you can accept a change to the plan.**

### 7. A short report every week
What's done, what's late, and what's next. It goes to you in chat and by push.

### What it never does alone
It never emails, pays, or writes into saifsys, and it never changes the plan without your tap.

### Money
- It uses **Sonnet 5.5 only** (no Haiku).
- Reading the file and writing the plan costs about **$3–5, once**.
- After that, Reem only thinks when someone sends an update, plus once a week for the report: a little each week.
- **Test limit: $5** for making the first plan. It stops and asks you before spending more.
- It works **7:00–22:00 Dubai time** only.
- All cost shows in Billing under "Project planner".

### Who
- Starts a plan: **only the master**.
- Updates: the people already in the project (**Francisca, Arzhel**).
- Late alerts: **master + Francisca + Arzhel**.

### Build in steps
| Step | What you get |
|---|---|
| 1 | One file in chat → Reem reads it and asks its questions → plan card → Start puts the steps into the Project, PDF on Shelf |
| 2 | Late checks against the plan + push to you, Francisca and Arzhel + "accept this change?" card |
| 3 | Weekly report |
| 4 | Rules: what you correct, it remembers for the next project |

First test: the JVC school, with your drawing file.

### Outside services
None.

## Part B: how it is built (for whoever builds it)

**Goal:** the master attaches one project file; Reem extracts the facts, asks for what's missing, writes a phased plan with quality checks, and, on Start, seeds the existing Project's steps. Afterwards it compares site updates against the plan and proposes changes the master accepts.

**Architecture:** its own module and tables (no flags on Projects). It reuses Projects for the live record: `project_steps` (weeks, hold points), `project_moves` (materials), `project_issues`/`blocked`, and the existing card flow in `project_proposals`. Site people change nothing here; they use Projects exactly as today.

**Files (as built)**
- `server/projectPlans.js`: everything server-side: reading the file, the facts, the plan, its checks, Start (merge into Progress), the plan PDF, the daily late check, the Sunday report, `planKit` (master only) and the routes under `/api/plans`.
  - Reading: a PDF with its own text (CAD exports) is read from that text, the title block repeated on every sheet kept once; long text is written down as notes in 90k-character pieces. A scanned PDF is sent to Claude as page pictures in groups under 12 MB. Anything else (DWG, Word) uses the text the Shelf already keeps.
  - The reading and planning run in the background, one plan at a time; the chat card polls `GET /api/plans/:id`. A restart marks a plan in progress as stopped, with Try again.
- Client: `PlanCard.jsx` (chat card: questions, plan, Start / Try again / Cancel / PDF), `ProjectPlan.jsx` (the project's Plan tab: late items with Accept new weeks / Keep the plan, weekly report, phases, materials, manpower, risks).
- No new module: only the master makes plans, and the Plan tab sits inside Projects (`needsModule('projects')`).
- Model: `ADVISOR_MODEL` (Sonnet 5.5) for reading, planning and checking; billed as the job `project_planner`.
- Timer: `startPlanWatch()`; the daily check and the report run where the project pushes run (production, or `PROJECTS_PUSH=on`).
- File size: the upload limit stays 25 MB for a PDF (35 MB for a DWG). Reading the text of a 34 MB drawing PDF took about 630 MB of memory, too close to what the live server has free.
- Tests: `tests/project-plans.test.js`; new tables in `TABLES` in `tests/helpers/db.js`.

### Tables (idempotent DDL in `server/db.js`)
```sql
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS project_plans (
  id SERIAL PRIMARY KEY,
  project_id INTEGER REFERENCES projects(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  user_id INTEGER NOT NULL REFERENCES users(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,   -- the master who made it
  document_id INTEGER,                       -- the one source file on the Shelf
  facts JSONB NOT NULL DEFAULT '{}',         -- what was read from the file
  answers JSONB NOT NULL DEFAULT '{}',       -- answers to the questions
  plan JSONB,                                -- phases → steps → checks, weeks, materials, manpower, risks
  status TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'reading' CHECK (status IN
    ('reading','asking','planned','started','cancelled','failed')),
  spent NUMERIC NOT NULL DEFAULT 0,
  spend_limit NUMERIC NOT NULL DEFAULT 5,
  report_doc_id INTEGER,
  started_at BIGINT,
  created_at BIGINT DEFAULT extract(epoch from now())::bigint
);
CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS plan_alerts (
  id SERIAL PRIMARY KEY,
  plan_id INTEGER NOT NULL REFERENCES project_plans(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  step_id INTEGER REFERENCES project_steps(id) ON DELETE CASCADE,
  kind TEXT NOT NULL CHECK (kind IN ('not_started','not_finished','material','unsigned','blocked')),
  text TEXT NOT NULL,
  change JSONB,                              -- proposed shift: [{step_id, week_from, week_to}]
  status TEXT NOT NULL DEFAULT 'open' CHECK (status IN ('open','accepted','declined','gone')),
  decided_by INTEGER REFERENCES users(id) ON DELETE SET NULL,
  created_at BIGINT DEFAULT extract(epoch from now())::bigint,
  UNIQUE (plan_id, step_id, kind)            -- one alert per step and reason, never repeated daily
);
```

### Flow
1. **Read.** `start_plan(document_id)` (master only, one file). `planRead` extracts the facts; the facts and the questions show on one card.
2. **Ask.** Answers (or more files) fill `answers`. Status `asking` → `planned` once the planner returns a valid plan JSON (schema-checked; refused and retried once, never saved half).
3. **Check.** Before the card is shown, a separate checker call grades each phase against its quality points and fixes what fails (max 2 rounds). Total cost is held under `spend_limit`; reaching it stops and asks the master.
4. **Start.** The master taps Start → in one `tx()`, create or link the project, write `project_steps` (floor, stage, weeks, hold point, checklist points) with `steps_seeded = true`, and put the PDF on the Shelf.
5. **Watch.** Once a day at 07:00 Dubai (plus right after a confirmed site card), `planWatch` compares `project_steps`, `project_moves` and blocked/issue records against the plan, and opens at most one `plan_alert` per step and reason. Pushes go to the master + the project's members. For an alert with a `change`, the master gets an accept/decline card; accepting updates the weeks of the affected steps in one `tx()`.
6. **Report.** Sunday 08:00 Dubai: a short weekly report to the master (chat + push).

### Tests
1. Only the master can start a plan or accept a change; members get 403; external users never see the module.
2. A plan JSON that fails the schema is never saved.
3. Start twice (two devices) → the steps are written once; the second tap gets 409.
4. The same late step doesn't raise a second alert the next day.
5. Accepting a change moves only the listed steps of that project.
6. Over `spend_limit` → no more AI calls for that plan until the master says go on.
7. The watch runs only within 7:00–22:00 Dubai and only with `PLAN_WATCH=on`.
